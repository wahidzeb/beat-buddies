About Beat Buddies

Beat Buddies is a music toy for kids. Drag cute, round buddies onto a stage and each one starts singing, drumming or beatboxing, always in time, so every mix sounds good.

There are 25 buddies to unlock with notes, several stages, Daily Fun tasks and 7 secret combos to discover. You can record a song of up to 2 minutes and share it with a link. There is no failing and no timer.

3 quick tips

  1. Tap a buddy on stage to Mute or Solo it, and hear how a song is built from layers.
  2. Try different groups of buddies together. The right mix sets off a secret combo, such as Starry Night.
  3. A share link stores the song inside the link itself. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

How to play

Drag a buddy from the bottom onto a spot on the stage, or just tap it. Each buddy plays its own part of the song, always in time.

Mix Beats, Bass, Melody, Chords, Voices and Effects. Up to 7 buddies can play at once!

Tap a buddy on stage to mute it, solo it, or send it home. You can also drag it off the stage.

Some buddies have a secret combo. Put the right friends together for a special dance and a bonus part of the song! Peek in the Buddy Book for clues.

Press record, make your song, press it again to stop. Then play it back or share a link so others can hear it.

Jamming earns notes. Use them to unlock new buddies and stages.
